# 💻 Agarwal Computer - Official Customer Lucky Draw Portal

A clean, responsive, and modern Lucky Draw presentation system built for in-store displays, laptops, tablets, and mobile screens at **Agarwal Computer**.

---

## 🎨 Brand Identity & Blue Theme
* **Logo**: Official Agarwal Computer stylized triangular icon with dynamic horizontal wing swoosh.
* **Typography**: Clean, bold geometric sans-serif (`Outfit` & `Rajdhani`) matching the provided brand font styling with drop-cap initials and underline strokes.
* **Colors**:
  * **#2563eb** / **#1d4ed8** — Electric Corporate Tech Blue *(Primary brand color, buttons, ribbon accents)*
  * **#38bdf8** / **#60a5fa** — Vibrant Cyan & Sky Blue *(Highlights, badges & subtle glows)*
  * **#090e1a** / **#0f172a** — Deep Obsidian Slate *(Modern tech background & glass cards)*

---

## 📱 Responsive & Screen-Fit Design
* **Compact Header**: Minimal 54px header with zero bloated tickers or filler bars.
* **Zero Scrolling on Laptops / Desktops**: The full 3x3 draw arena fits completely inside standard 768px/800px laptop viewports.
* **Touch-Friendly on Mobile**: Clean 3-column responsive grid scaling seamlessly down to smartphones.
* **No "Vibe-Coded" Clutter**: Removed tacky casino gold ribbons, antique serif fonts, and filler 3-column footers in favor of an authentic, trustworthy tech-retail aesthetic.

---

## 📺 Lucky Draw Workflow
1. **Invoice & Mobile Entry**: Enter registered phone & bill number.
2. **Smooth Zoom-In**: Selecting any of the 9 gift boxes smoothly centers the camera onto that box.
3. **3D Unboxing & Blue Sprinklers**: Box flips in 3D with celebration confetti.
4. **"Show All Boxes" Dock**: Button appears directly under the opened box.
5. **Vanishing Button ➔ Zoom-Out ➔ Sequential Reveal**: Clicking reveals the other 8 boxes one-by-one with realistic animations.
6. **"Start New Draw"**: Clean reset card pops up to welcome the next customer.
